Airfoil Lab

How a 2-D wing section behaves — the lift, drag, and pitching moment it makes as the angle of attack changes.

What it shows
  • Cl, Cd, Cm vs α, the drag polar, and L/D
  • the section pitched to the live α
  • best L/D, Cl,max, and the low-drag "bucket"
How to use it
  • pick from 2,000+ airfoils (start typing to search) or type any naca####
  • set the Reynolds number and the α sweep, then Compute
  • drag the α slider to move the operating point in real time
Method

Polars come from NeuralFoil, a machine-learning surrogate of XFOIL — viscous, low-speed (incompressible), Reynolds-based. Results are engineering estimates and lose accuracy past stall.

Try it: sweep α past ~12° and watch drag climb as lift rolls over into stall.

Summary
Powered by NeuralFoil -- a machine-learning surrogate of XFOIL (viscous, steady, 2-D, incompressible). Reynolds is chord-based; results are engineering estimates for teaching and lose accuracy past stall. Compressibility (Mach) is a planned enhancement.
