Wing / VLM

From a 2-D section to a whole 3-D wing — how the planform shapes the lift, the induced drag, and the wing's stability.

What it shows
  • spanwise lift distribution vs the ideal elliptical
  • CL, induced CDi, and span efficiency e
  • stability derivatives, the neutral point, and static margin
  • an interactive 3-D wing coloured by local lift
How to use it
  • set the planform: span, chord, taper, sweep, dihedral, twist
  • pick an airfoil and angle of attack
  • enter a CG (%MAC) to get the static margin
  • drag the 3-D wing to orbit, scroll to zoom
Method

Vortex-lattice (AeroSandbox), inviscid — it captures lift, induced drag, and stability, not profile drag. Pair with Airfoil Lab for the section's viscous drag.

Try it: compare taper 1.0 (rectangular) vs 0.4 and watch e and the loading change; then move the CG aft and watch the static margin shrink.
